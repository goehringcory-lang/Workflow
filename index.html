How it works

Once, 20 minutes

Fill in one fact sheet

Your trips, prices, policies and how you sound. Write "none" for no, leave a line blank if undecided. Every kit reads from this one sheet.

Each time

Use ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. A free account works. Paste the prompt, your fact sheet and the email or review.

You decide

Read, fix, send

Anything your sheet does not answer comes back as a short list for you to confirm, not a guess. Nothing is sent for you.

Guest Reply Desk for Gmail

A Chrome extension in early testing. Open a guest email in Gmail, click Draft reply, and get a draft built from your own documents and your booking calendar. It flags any number it cannot trace back to your documents, holds anything about an injury or a complaint for you, and never says a trip is sold out unless the calendar says so. It never sends email.

Not available yet. It has not been run on a real Gmail account or a live booking system.

What has been tested

  • Every prompt has been run on Claude with a fictional business. The worked examples in each kit are those runs, word for word.
  • Not yet run on ChatGPT or Gemini.
  • All businesses and people in the examples are made up.
  • These are drafting aids, not legal advice. Remove guest surnames, phone numbers and booking numbers before you paste anything.
