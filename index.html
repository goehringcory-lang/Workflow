Guest Reply Desk for Chrome (Gmail & Any Inbox)

A bring-your-own-key Chrome extension for tour, outdoor, and education operators. Open a guest email in Gmail—or paste any Outlook, Apple Mail, or HelpScout thread into the Side Panel—and get a draft built from your own PDFs/Word docs and your booking calendar.

Any calendar, zero vendor gatekeeping

Connect a read-only iCal (.ics) calendar feed, upload a CSV departure schedule, or enable experimental direct connectors (Bokun, Checkfront, Rezdy, Peek, Xola, FareHarbor).

Your AI account

Deterministic trace checks

Code—not the AI—checks dates and party size against your calendar, flags any price or time not found in your docs, and holds injuries or complaints. Never clicks Send.

What has—and has not—been tested

  • Every prompt packet has been run on Claude with a fictional business and graded against 38 planted safety, policy, and arithmetic traps. The worked examples are those runs, word for word.
  • Live ChatGPT and Gemini benchmark runs are pending; an open trap-grading harness is ready so results can be published transparently once run.
  • The deterministic Season Export Check has been verified in code against our 384-row synthetic booking export ground truth (catching all 6 hidden duplicates and 3 test bookings).
  • All businesses and people in the examples are fictional.
  • These are drafting aids, not legal or accounting advice. Remove guest surnames, phone numbers, and booking numbers before pasting anything into an AI chat.
