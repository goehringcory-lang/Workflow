# Business Fact Sheet (fill in once, used by every packet)

Every packet reads from this one sheet. Fill it in once, save it as a note or document, and paste it wherever a prompt says `[PASTE YOUR FACT SHEET]`. Setup takes about 20 minutes the first time.

## Three rules for filling it in

1. **Write only what is true today.** The assistant treats this sheet as the only source of prices, ages, times and policies. A wrong line here becomes a wrong line in an email.
2. **"None" and blank mean different things.** Write `none` when the answer is no (for example, "Group discounts: none"). Leave a line blank when you have not decided. A blank line makes the assistant say "we will confirm" and put the question on your OWNER TO CONFIRM list instead of guessing.
3. **No guest data.** Nothing on this sheet should name a guest.

## Worked example (fictional business used in every packet's example)

```text
BUSINESS FACT SHEET
Business name and town: Tamarack Ridge Guides, Bishop, California (fictional)
Owner or manager (first name): Dana
Private contact for guests (email or phone): dana@tamarackridge.example
Today's date (update when you paste): November 21, 2026

TRIPS (one line per trip)
- Half-Day Naturalist Walk: $95 adult, $65 youth (8-15). Minimum age 8. Max 12 guests. 3 miles, mostly flat. Starts 9:00 am, about 3.5 hours. Meets at the Buttermilk Road trailhead lot. Runs April to October. Guests bring water and snacks.
- Full-Day Lakes Basin Hike: $165 per person. Minimum age 12. Max 10. 8 miles, 1,600 ft of climbing, rocky trail. Starts 8:00 am, about 7 hours. Meets at the South Lake trailhead lot. Runs May to October. Guests bring their own lunch.
- Winter Snowshoe: $120 per person, snowshoes and poles included. Minimum age 10. Max 10. 2 miles on packed snow. Starts 10:00 am, about 3 hours. Meets at the Rock Creek Sno-Park lot. Runs Dec 1 to Mar 31.
- 3-Day Backpacking Intro: $690 per person. Minimum age 14. Max 8. Guests carry 30-35 lb packs. Camps at 10,200 ft. Meals on trail included. Runs May to October.

PRIVATE AND CUSTOM TRIPS
- Pricing rule: Private Custom Day is $540 for up to 4 guests, $85 for each extra guest.
- Max group size and minimum age: up to 10 guests, minimum age 8.
- Start times we offer: 8:00 am or 9:00 am.
- Route options:
  A. Buttermilk loop: 3 miles, mostly flat, about 3.5 hours. Suits: first-time hikers and mixed groups.
  B. Lower Lakes: 5 miles, 700 ft of climbing, about 5 hours including a lunch stop. Suits: mixed groups who walk regularly.
  C. Lakes Basin full route: 8 miles, 1,600 ft of climbing, rocky, about 7 hours. Suits: fit, experienced hikers.

POLICIES
- Guest cancels: full refund 14 or more days out; 50% refund 7-13 days out; no refund inside 7 days.
- We cancel (weather): full refund or free reschedule, guest's choice.
- Smoke or poor air quality:
- Dogs: not allowed.
- Transportation: not provided. Guests drive to the meeting place.
- Gratuity: not included.

COMMON QUESTIONS
- Group discounts: none.
- Other discounts (military, local, nonprofit, repeat guest):
- Accessibility: We describe the trail honestly and let guests decide. For specific needs, guests can email Dana.
- Deposits and payment terms: scheduled trips are paid in full at booking. Private days take a 50% deposit, balance due 14 days before the trip.
- Invoicing a company: yes, for private days, on request.
- Lunch and food: not provided on day trips. Guests bring their own.
- Tax and fees:
- Waivers: every guest signs online before the trip; a parent or guardian signs for anyone under 18.

VOICE
- How we sound: warm, plain, short sentences.
- Greeting and sign-off we use: "Hi [first name]," and "The Tamarack Ridge crew".
- Things we never do: more than one exclamation mark per message, emoji, sales talk.

VOICE SAMPLES
Sample 1: "Hi [name], Thanks for writing. The Naturalist Walk is a good pick for a first morning here. It is 3 miles and mostly flat, and we start at 9:00. Bring water and a snack. We will check the calendar for the 14th and write back today. The Tamarack Ridge crew"
Sample 2: "Hi [name], Good question. Snowshoes and poles are on us. Wear waterproof boots and bring a warm layer you can take off. See you at the Sno-Park lot. The Tamarack Ridge crew"
```

Three lines are blank on purpose in the example (smoke, other discounts, tax). The packet examples show what the assistant does when it meets them.
